Privacy Policy

Your window information stays on your Mac.

This policy explains how SplitWindow handles visible window information, app settings, purchases, and macOS permissions.

Effective date: September 7, 2026

Overview

SplitWindow is a macOS utility that arranges application windows into layouts. It does not require an account and does not operate a cloud service for processing window content.

SplitWindow does not sell personal information and does not include advertising or third-party analytics.

Window information

To show eligible windows and arrange the windows you select, SplitWindow reads information made available by macOS, including application name, application icon, window title, window position and size, process identifier, and system window identifier.

This information is processed locally for the current layout operation. Runtime window references and identifiers are not uploaded to a SplitWindow-operated server and are not persisted as a history of your activity.

Accessibility permission

Accessibility permission is required to move, resize, focus, and raise windows belonging to other applications. SplitWindow uses this permission only to perform the window-management actions you request.

You can review or revoke this permission at any time in System Settings > Privacy & Security > Accessibility. Without it, SplitWindow cannot apply layouts to other applications.

Screen Recording permission

Screen Recording permission is optional. When granted, SplitWindow uses Apple's ScreenCaptureKit to create static previews of eligible windows while you choose which windows belong in a layout.

Preview images are processed locally and used only for the active interface. SplitWindow does not record video, save these previews as user files, or upload them. If permission is not granted, the app shows application icons and names instead.

Information stored on your Mac

SplitWindow stores app preferences locally, including layout spacing, screen margins, remembered layout proportions, and configured keyboard shortcuts. These settings do not contain captured window images or a history of window titles.

Removing the app may not automatically remove preferences retained by macOS.

Purchases

Subscriptions and the lifetime unlock are processed by Apple through StoreKit and the Mac App Store. Apple may process payment information, purchase identifiers, and transaction records under Apple's own privacy policy.

SplitWindow reads StoreKit product and entitlement information needed to display purchase options, restore purchases, and unlock Pro layouts. SplitWindow does not receive your full payment card details.

Network access and sharing

SplitWindow does not transmit window titles, screenshots, layout contents, or app preferences to a SplitWindow-operated server. Network access may occur when macOS and StoreKit communicate with Apple to load products, complete a purchase, or refresh entitlements.

Children's privacy

SplitWindow is not directed to children under 13 and does not provide accounts, social features, or targeted advertising.

Policy changes

This policy may be updated when SplitWindow's features or data practices change. The effective date above will be revised when material changes are published.
