Privacy Policy

Your images stay on your Mac.

This policy explains how ImageTrans handles source images, local AI processing, exported files, settings, and purchases.

Effective date: September 10, 2026

Overview

ImageTrans is a macOS image conversion and editing application. It does not require an account and does not operate a cloud service for image processing.

Images and folders

ImageTrans accesses only images, folders, and export locations that you explicitly select or drag into the app. Source images and GIF frames are read locally and are not uploaded to an ImageTrans-operated server.

Local image and AI processing

Format conversion, compression, resizing, GIF frame extraction, brightness and contrast adjustments, masks, background removal, and clarity enhancement run on your Mac. Background removal uses Apple Vision, and clarity enhancement uses a bundled Core ML model. Image pixels are not sent to an external AI service.

Exports and temporary files

ImageTrans creates only the output files you request. It may create temporary files while processing and removes them after completion or cancellation when supported by macOS. Exported files may not retain every item of source metadata, including EXIF or GPS metadata.

Settings stored on your Mac

ImageTrans may store app preferences and purchase entitlement state locally. It does not store an online history of the images you edit or convert.

Purchases and network access

Subscriptions and the lifetime unlock are processed by Apple through StoreKit and the Mac App Store. Apple may process payment and transaction information under Apple's privacy policy. ImageTrans receives product and entitlement information needed to display purchase options, complete purchases, restore purchases, and unlock features; it does not receive your full payment card details. Network access may occur when StoreKit communicates with Apple.

Analytics, advertising, and sharing

ImageTrans does not include third-party advertising or analytics SDKs, does not sell personal information, and does not share your image content with third parties.

Children's privacy

ImageTrans is not directed to children under 13 and does not provide accounts, social features, or targeted advertising.

Policy changes

This policy may be updated if ImageTrans features or data practices change. The effective date will be revised when material changes are published.
