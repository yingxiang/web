Privacy Policy

Keyboard Garden · Privacy Policy

Overview

No account registration is required. The app includes no third-party advertising or analytics SDKs. The developer does not collect or upload your typed text.

Input Monitoring

With your permission, the app reads supported physical letter and number key events to count individual keys and drive growth and effects. It does not record text, passwords, document contents or keystroke sequences. Secure Input may prevent events from being delivered.

Local data

Crop and fish selections, growth, harvests, daily and per-key counts, and preferences are stored on your Mac. Switching scenes or changing purchase entitlements does not delete existing growth or harvest records.

Purchases and network access

Apple StoreKit and App Store services handle prices, purchase verification, subscriptions and lifetime entitlements. Apple may process transaction and necessary network information under its own privacy policy. The app reads transaction information to unlock content; the developer does not receive full payment card details. Key statistics and typed content are not sent to the developer with purchase requests.

Resources and sharing

Scene and animation assets ship with the app; no remote asset downloads are needed. Images are exported only when you save, copy or share them. A third-party sharing service handles shared images and text under its own policy.

Data controls and updates

You can revoke Input Monitoring in System Settings. Back up local data before deleting it; deleting data does not cancel subscriptions. This policy’s effective date will change if data handling changes.
