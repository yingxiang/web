隐私政策

Privacy Policy · Updated October 24, 2026

本地运行 / Local operation

MochiBuddy 无需注册账号。应用不收集或向开发者传输个人信息，不包含广告或第三方分析 SDK，也不追踪你的使用行为。

MochiBuddy requires no account. The app does not collect or transmit personal data to the developer, include advertising or third-party analytics SDKs, or track your usage.

保存在设备上的内容 / On-device settings

团子的样式、颜色、大小、手感、位置和语言偏好通过 macOS 本地偏好设置保存在你的 Mac 上，用于恢复应用状态。你可以在应用中移除团子或修改设置。

Companion appearance, color, size, material, positions and language preferences are stored locally using macOS preferences to restore app state. You can remove companions or change settings in the app.

桌面与权限 / Desktop and permissions

应用不读取或录制屏幕内容，不访问你的文档、相机或麦克风。互动时会读取鼠标位置和按键状态，以响应拖拽；这些信息不会发送给开发者。

The app does not read or record screen content or access your documents, camera or microphone. Pointer location and button state are used locally to respond to interactions and are not sent to the developer.

支持邮件 / Support emails

如果你主动通过邮件联系支持，我们会收到你提供的邮箱地址和邮件内容，仅用于处理你的请求。请勿发送不必要的敏感信息。需要删除支持邮件时，可使用下方邮箱联系我们。

If you email support, we receive the address and content you provide to handle your request. Please avoid unnecessary sensitive information. Contact us below to request deletion of support correspondence.

网站和外部服务 / Website and external services

本网站不设置分析或广告 Cookie。网站托管服务可能处理 IP 地址等常规访问日志。下载链接会打开 Apple App Store；Apple 对下载和交易信息的处理适用其隐私政策。

This site sets no analytics or advertising cookies. The hosting provider may process standard access logs such as IP addresses. Download links open the Apple App Store, where Apple's privacy policy governs downloads and transactions.

政策更新与联系 / Updates and contact

如数据处理方式发生变化，我们会更新本页。隐私问题请联系 xiangying320@126.com。

We will update this page if our data practices change. For privacy questions, email the address above.
