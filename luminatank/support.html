LuminaTank · Support

Getting started

Requires macOS 14 or later. Launch LuminaTank to display the aquarium on your desktop. Open the aquarium console from the menu bar or click the Dock icon to choose fish, plants, decorations and equipment.

Feeding and mouse interaction

While the app runs, mouse clicks on the main display automatically drop food, and cursor movement makes nearby fish scatter. Console clicks operate the controls instead of feeding. If nothing happens, check that the app is running and return to the main desktop; some system contexts may affect event delivery.

Fish growth

Fish gain growth points when they eat pellets. They have up to 10 growth levels, becoming larger and showing bubble and particle effects as they level up. A click alone does not mean a fish has eaten. Choose from 16 ornamental fish varieties, cherry shrimp and 20 aquatic plants.

Decorations, lighting and oxygen

Use the console to arrange plants, coral and rocks and adjust sand style and height. Color lighting offers color, brightness and switching controls. Oxygen equipment affects oxygen status, and food options set how much food each click drops.

Missing scene or console

Confirm the app is running and return to the main display’s desktop. Windows, full-screen apps or other desktop spaces may cover the aquarium. Open the console from the menu bar or Dock. Closing the console keeps the aquarium running; use Quit in the menu bar to stop it.

Saves and support

Aquarium state saves locally and restores on reopening. Back up saves before changing Macs, reinstalling the system or deleting data.
