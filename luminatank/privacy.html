LuminaTank · Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 27, 2026

Overview

LuminaTank requires no account and includes no third-party advertising or analytics SDKs. The current app does not upload aquarium saves or mouse activity to developer servers.

Mouse interaction

While running, the app processes macOS pointer positions, movement and click events locally to feed fish, trigger avoidance and show effects. It does not store cursor trails or click histories. It does not monitor keyboard input or read typed text, passwords, other apps’ documents or screen contents. Saved feeding totals do not include individual click locations or timestamps.

Local aquarium data

Fish types, names, growth and health, plant and decoration placements, sand, lighting, oxygen equipment, feeding totals, simulated balance and unlocked items are saved in the local LuminaTank Application Support directory. Some preferences use local system settings. macOS manages the location in sandboxed builds. The current app has no account or cloud sync.

Network and external services

Aquarium and animation assets ship with the app; everyday use does not require downloading assets from developer servers. When you open the App Store, Apple’s terms or your email app, those services handle information you submit under their own policies. The developer does not receive payment card details through this app.

Data controls and support

Quitting stops mouse event processing. To remove saves, quit, back up your data and delete the relevant local LuminaTank save; removing the app alone may leave local data behind. Support emails and attachments you choose to send are used to reply and investigate your issue. Do not send passwords, payment information or unrelated personal data.

Website and policy updates

These pages use no advertising, analytics scripts or tracking cookies. A language preference may be saved in browser local storage; you can clear it in your browser. The hosting provider may process access logs needed to serve the site. We will update this policy and effective date if data handling changes.
