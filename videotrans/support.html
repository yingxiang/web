Technical Support

Help with VideoTrans

Troubleshooting for MP4-to-MOV conversion, local background removal, transparent output, audio, frame rate, size, and codec settings.

Common questions

Conversion appears stuck

VideoTrans processes each frame locally. ProRes 4444 encoding can take longer and produce large files. For faster, smaller output, choose HEVC Alpha with Compact or Standard quality. If progress does not change for several minutes, cancel and retry with audio disabled.

The transparent video looks black

Some ordinary players preview transparent pixels as black. Verify the Alpha channel in a video editor that supports transparent HEVC or ProRes 4444.

Which codec should I choose?

Use HEVC Alpha for smaller transparent files, ProRes 4444 for professional editing compatibility, and HEVC or H.264 when transparency is not needed.

Audio is missing

Enable Keep Audio before exporting. VideoTrans merges the original audio after the transparent video is written. Some source audio formats may not be supported by the MOV container.

Frame-rate or size is unavailable

The frame-rate menu is hidden for sources at 5 fps or below. Available rates never exceed the source frame rate. The size menu offers the original and half-size dimensions.

Before requesting support

  • Install the latest VideoTrans and macOS updates available to you.
  • Record the VideoTrans version, macOS version, source codec, and selected export options.
  • Try a short sample video and note the exact progress stage or error message.
