Privacy Policy

Your videos stay on your Mac.

This policy explains how VideoTrans handles video files, audio, local processing, and exported results.

Effective date: August 6, 2026

Overview

VideoTrans is a macOS video conversion tool. It converts supported video files to MOV and can remove backgrounds locally using Apple Vision. Video and audio content are processed on your Mac.

Video and audio files

VideoTrans accesses files you explicitly select or drag into the app. It reads the source file to perform the conversion and writes the result to a temporary location before presenting it for export. The app does not upload your media to a VideoTrans-operated server.

Background removal

When enabled, background removal uses Apple Vision and Core Image on your Mac. Frames are not sent to an external recognition or artificial-intelligence service.

Exported files

VideoTrans creates the MOV file you request, including optional transparency, audio, frame-rate, size, and codec settings. Temporary intermediate files are removed after a successful export when supported by the operating system.

Analytics and advertising

  • VideoTrans does not require account registration.
  • The current version does not include third-party advertising or analytics SDKs.
  • VideoTrans does not sell personal information or video content.

Policy changes

This policy may be updated if VideoTrans adds online services or changes how information is handled. The effective date will be revised when material changes are published.
