Changelog

What's new in Maple

A concise history of recent screenshot, annotation, OCR, and workflow improvements.

26.4.2

Expanded capture tools for color sampling and document workflows.

  • Added live on-screen color sampling with quick color-value copying.
  • Added scrolling screenshots with seamless document stitching.

26.4.1

Improved annotation editing and general interaction quality.

  • Fixed an issue that prevented text annotations from rotating.
  • Refined the overall editing experience.

26.4.0

Expanded mosaic, OCR, and custom style capabilities.

  • Added a mosaic entry to the toolbar and multiple Gaussian blur levels.
  • Added OCR recognition and text extraction.
  • Added importing, saving, and deleting custom SVG styles.

26.3.0

Broadened annotation coverage and editing controls.

  • Added brush, text, and shape tools.
  • Added deletion, rotation, and scaling for annotations.
  • Added color, line width, and opacity controls.
