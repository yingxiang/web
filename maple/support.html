Technical Support

Help with Maple

Troubleshooting guidance for screen capture, scrolling screenshots, OCR, annotations, saving, and purchases on macOS.

What Maple supports

Maple provides area capture, scrolling screenshots, OCR and text extraction, annotation tools, mosaic redaction, color sampling, and direct copy, save, or drag-and-drop output.

Common questions

Screen capture does not start

Open System Settings and confirm that Maple has Screen Recording permission. After changing the permission, quit and reopen Maple before trying again.

Scrolling capture cannot control the page

Confirm that Maple has Accessibility permission. Keep the target window visible and avoid switching apps while the scrolling capture is running.

OCR misses or misreads text

Use a clear screenshot with sufficient resolution and contrast. Recognition quality can vary with small text, decorative fonts, rotation, mixed languages, and compressed images.

A copied or saved image cannot be found

Repeat the output action and confirm the selected destination. When dragging into another app, make sure that app accepts image drops and remains active until the transfer completes.

A purchase is not recognized

Confirm that the Mac App Store uses the Apple Account that made the purchase, then open the unlock page and restore purchases.

Before requesting support

  • Install the latest Maple and macOS updates available to you.
  • Verify Screen Recording and Accessibility permissions in System Settings.
  • Restart Maple and repeat the operation once.
  • Record the Maple version, macOS version, and exact reproduction steps.
