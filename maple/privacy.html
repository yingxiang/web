Privacy Policy

Your screenshots stay on your Mac.

This policy explains how Maple handles screen content, OCR, files, purchases, and macOS permissions.

Effective date: July 24, 2026

Overview

Maple is a macOS screenshot, annotation, OCR, and redaction app. Screenshot content and annotation data are primarily processed on your device.

Screen Recording permission

Maple requests macOS Screen Recording permission only for screenshot features and workflows you actively trigger. The permission is not used to upload your screen content to a Maple-operated server.

Accessibility permission

Maple requests Accessibility permission for scrolling screenshots you actively trigger. It is used while performing the scrolling action and is not used to collect or upload unrelated content.

OCR and annotations

OCR, text extraction, shapes, text notes, mosaic redaction, and related editing operations are processed locally. The current version does not upload this content to an external OCR service.

Files, copy, and drag

When you save, copy, or drag content, Maple uses the data only to complete the action you requested. The result remains under your control on your device or in the destination app you choose.

Purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple through StoreKit and the App Store. Maple reads transaction and entitlement information needed to unlock features but does not receive your full payment card details.

Analytics and advertising

  • Maple does not require account registration.
  • The current version does not include third-party advertising SDKs for tracking.
  • Maple does not intentionally collect screenshot contents for profiling.
  • Maple does not sell personal information.

Policy changes

This policy may be updated if Maple adds online services or changes how information is handled. The effective date will be revised when material changes are published.
